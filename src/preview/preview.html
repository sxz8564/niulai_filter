Using it in Google Meet

  1. Open or reload meet.google.com — the filter hooks the camera as the page loads, so a tab that was already open needs a reload.
  2. Join the meeting and turn your camera on. Your self-view shows the animal head, and that is exactly what everyone else receives.
  3. Adjust from the toolbar popup at any time; changes apply live, no rejoin needed.
  4. If the camera was already running when you changed something big, toggle the camera off and on in Meet.

Also works on Zoom web, Microsoft Teams, Webex, Whereby, Discord and Gather. Native desktop apps cannot be filtered — use the browser version of the meeting.
